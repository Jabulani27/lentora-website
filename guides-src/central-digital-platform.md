When the Procurement Act 2023 went live on **24 February 2025**, it brought a new piece of plumbing with it: the **central digital platform**. If you have heard the term and weren't sure what it means for your business, here is the plain-English version.

## At a glance

- The central digital platform launched on **24 February 2025**, alongside the Procurement Act 2023.
- Its public face is the **enhanced Find a Tender service**.
- It is **free to use for everyone**.
- Suppliers register **once** and reuse their core business details across multiple bids.
- You only need to register **when you want to bid** — there is no rush.

## What is the central digital platform?

The central digital platform is the government's online system that underpins the new procurement regime. For suppliers, the part you interact with is the **enhanced Find a Tender service** — where public bodies publish their notices and where you register your organisation.

The big idea is "tell us once". Instead of re-entering the same company information on every portal for every bid, you store your **core business details** centrally and reuse them. That is meant to cut the repetitive form-filling that has long frustrated smaller suppliers.

## How suppliers register

Registration runs through **GOV.UK One Login**, then on into the Central Digital Platform. The government describes it as a few straightforward steps to register your organisation and set up your first administrator.

When you register, you will typically need:

- Your organisation's **name and address**
- Your **Companies House number** (or equivalent, such as a charity number)
- Your **VAT number**, if applicable
- Any relevant **qualifications or trade assurances**
- Whether you are an **SME, VCSE or public mutual**

To complete a bid, you may also be asked for:

- Copies of your **accounts** from the most recent financial years
- Names and addresses of any **connected persons** (for example, people with significant control)
- Details of any **exclusions** that apply to you or connected persons, as set out in Schedules 6 and 7 of the Procurement Act 2023

You will also be given a **unique identifier** for your organisation as part of registration.

## Do I have to re-register if I used the old Find a Tender?

Yes. The service was enhanced as part of the move to the new regime, and existing users need to **re-register** on the new platform.

## When should I register?

There is no deadline and no hurry — you only need to register **at the point you want to bid** for a contract. Many suppliers choose to register early so their details are ready to go when the right opportunity appears.

## Registering is the easy bit

Registration is genuinely straightforward — an hour of admin, once. What follows is the harder part, and it is worth being clear-eyed about it before you start.

The central digital platform is where the newest above-threshold notices are published, but contracts are still spread across **Contracts Finder**, **Public Contracts Scotland** and **Sell2Wales** as well. Finding the right one is a monitoring job. Winning it is a writing job: a competitive submission for a £100k contract is typically several thousand words of method statement, social value response and quality answers, against a published scoring matrix, to a fixed deadline.

Plenty of capable suppliers register, look at that, and never submit. If that sounds like where you are heading, bidding alongside a prime who writes the submission is worth considering.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Central Digital Platform the same as Find a Tender?
The enhanced **Find a Tender service** is the public-facing part of the central digital platform. In everyday use, suppliers register and search there.

### Does it cost anything to register?
No. Find a Tender and registration are free to use for everyone.

### What do I need to register?
At minimum your organisation's name, address, Companies House (or charity) number, VAT number if applicable, and your SME/VCSE status. To bid you may also need accounts, connected-person details and exclusion declarations.

### I registered on the old Find a Tender — do I need to do it again?
Yes, existing users need to re-register on the enhanced service.
