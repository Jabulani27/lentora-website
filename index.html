The Thin Prime Model

Lentora operates as a thin prime contractor: we win and manage government contracts, orchestrating delivery through a curated network of specialist SME subcontractors. This model gives public sector buyers the assurance of a single accountable prime with the agility and expertise of best-in-class delivery partners.

Our co-founders bring deep experience in programme management, compliance, and professional bid writing. We handle the governance, reporting, risk management, and contractual framework. Our suppliers focus on what they do best: delivering outstanding technical outcomes.

The result is lower cost, higher quality, and broader SME participation in public sector procurement.

For Suppliers

We are actively building our delivery partner network. If you are an SME with government technology experience, we want to hear from you.

  • Access to a managed bid pipeline — we identify, qualify, and write the bids. You focus on delivery.
  • Programme management wrapper — governance, reporting, and client management handled by Lentora.
  • Compliance and contractual support — we manage the prime contract, subcontract terms, and flow-down obligations.
  • Lower barrier to government work — join bids as a named subcontractor without the overhead of priming contracts yourself.
  • Fair commercial terms — transparent margin structure, back-to-back payment terms, and bid-specific exclusivity only.
  • Grow your government references — every successful delivery builds your track record for future opportunities.
